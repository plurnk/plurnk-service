The card describes NOTE as the register of every plan, finding, decision
and next action, and shows each of the four as a labelled example; its loop
caution names the Open Work a loop waits on. A continuing turn that carried
no NOTE is told so in the next packet by the `continued_without_note` notice,
`YOU MUST emit at least one NOTE in every turn.`
({§continued-without-note}); a turn of NOTEs alone draws no notice, and
`noted_without_operation` is gone. Each NOTE example's placeholder
names what that NOTE holds: the steps ahead and why, a finding and its
evidence, a decision and its reasons, the next action and what it needs. A
model worker's initialization program opens its surveys with a numbered plan,
labelled `<!-- plan -->` as the card's first NOTE example is, and closes them
with a second NOTE, labelled `<!-- next -->` as the card's last is and reading
`Read and resolve the Open Message.` ({§worker-initialization-entry}).
Loop Protocol opens with a tip: YOU SHOULD record every plan, finding,
decision and next action in detailed NOTEs to avoid permanent loss of
information. The card's first instruction and the
`invalid_emission` directive both name the fenced operation line in lowercase.
