A result whose receipt holds a body carries the receipt as a text part and the
body as a document part, served at `GET /agui/<workspace>/<worker>/log/L/T/S/OP`.
A `plurnk.ambient` row carries its body's URL beside a lean receipt, and no
receipt carries the model's line anchors ({§agui-body-reference}).
