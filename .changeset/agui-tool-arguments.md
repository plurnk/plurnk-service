A tool call's arguments are the operation as authored: its durable statement,
every operand exactly as parsed, without the verb that is the tool's name or the
position it was written at. The host, port, query and fragment, the scope, the
pattern, the aside and both COPY/MOVE selections now reach a standard AG-UI
client, live and on snapshot alike, where they were rebuilt from scheme and
path alone ({§agui-projection}).
