A turn whose final emission the output allowance cut is no longer judged a
cycle ({§engine-cycle-evidence}). It is excluded from the cycle history and
closes the window as a park does ({§loop-rail-continuity}), so turns cut before
any text no longer end the loop at 508 as "repeated" responses. Such turns
strike as before, and a threshold crossed on one is 500, naming the allowance
and its grant ({§output-allowance-notice}).
