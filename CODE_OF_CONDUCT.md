# Code of Conduct

We want participation in PLURNK to be constructive, welcoming, and safe.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and specific when discussing people, code, and decisions.
- Assume good intent while addressing concrete impact.
- Welcome questions and differing levels of experience.
- Give and accept technical criticism without personal attacks.
- Protect private information and respect reasonable boundaries.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate
intimidation, and sustained disruptive behavior are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through their GitHub
profile. Reports will be reviewed in context and handled as privately as
practical. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, issue warnings,
temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban participation when
necessary.

This policy applies in project repositories, discussions, review channels, and
other spaces where someone is representing the project.
